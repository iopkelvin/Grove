App Manual

Grove brings your tasks, study progress, friends, rooms, and streaks into one place so you can plan your work and stay consistent.

On this page Getting started Home dashboard Tasks Friends Study rooms Streaks Profile Troubleshooting

Getting started

Sign up with your name, email, and password, or log in with an existing account. After your first successful login, Grove presents a short guided tour over the Home dashboard.

The tutorial can be replayed later from Settings.

Home dashboard

Home is your overview page. It brings together your streak, online friends, calendar, upcoming activity, and current tasks.

  • Use the menu button in the upper-left corner to change pages.
  • Check the streak area to see your consistency.
  • Review the right-side cards for upcoming work and tasks.

Tasks

The Tasks page is backed by Grove's task API. Add a title, press Enter or the plus button, and use the checkbox to mark a task complete. Completed work may update your streak.

  • Create a focused task with a short, clear title.
  • Mark it complete using the square checkbox.
  • Delete tasks you no longer need using the remove button.

Friends

Use Friends to find other Grove users, send or respond to friend requests, and see the people connected to your account. Pending requests are also shown as a badge in the navigation menu.

Study rooms

Rooms provide shared spaces for focused study. Open the Rooms page, enter an available room, and use the room interface to work alongside other participants.

Streaks

Grove uses streaks to represent consistent task completion. Visit the Streaks page for a larger view of your progress and tree.

Profile

Your profile displays your Grove identity and account information. Other users can also be opened through their username-based profile link.

Troubleshooting

The tutorial does not appear

It starts only immediately after a successful login and normally appears once per user in that browser. Open Settings and choose Replay the Home tutorial to run it again.

I see an invalid refresh-token error

The browser may contain an old Supabase session. Clear the site's stored data, reload Grove, and log in again.

A page returns to Home

Confirm that the page has a real route in App.jsx rather than a redirect route.
